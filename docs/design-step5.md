# Step 5 design: driver-based planning (three statements)

Scope: `docs/SCOPE.md` job 4, first slice, following the original brief. Finance enters assumptions and the engine rolls them through P&L, balance sheet and cash flow by month, with checks that it ties every month. This slice was **12 months, one entity, one currency, base plus named scenarios**. Planning part two (`docs/design-planning-2.md`) adds the 36-month horizon with fiscal years and the plan as a budget submission; the rows below say where it changed a rule. AI-led setup, PVM bridges and the workforce module beyond a simple roster are later slices. Expected numbers: `docs/fixture-expected.md`, section "Step 5".

## Rules

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Money | Decimal. Each computed line (per product per month, per hire per month, tax, interest, depreciation) is rounded to cents, ROUND_HALF_UP, where it is created. Every total is a sum of rounded lines, so the balance sheet ties to the cent with no plug. Units are quantities, not money: they keep full precision and are shown as quantities (2,000; up to two decimals in the UI, whole units in the xlsx with the exact value in the cell). |
| Remainders | Two places split an amount into cent parts. **Settlement split**: an amount settled by a curve (or an opening AR/AP balance by its schedule) is split share by share, each part rounded to cents, and the **last share takes the remainder**, so the parts always add back to the amount. **Straight-line depreciation**: the monthly charge is cost (or opening net book value) ÷ life, rounded to cents, and the **last month of the life takes the remainder**, so the asset depreciates to exactly zero. |
| Horizon | `months` consecutive months from `first_month`, 1 to 36. **Fiscal years** start in `setup.fiscal_year_start` (a month number; default the month of `first_month`) and are labelled by the calendar year they start in (FY2027); a plan that starts or ends mid-year has part years. Year 1 (the first twelve months) is shown by month plus one column per fiscal year (`fct plan run --months all` and the UI's Show every month give every month; the xlsx and the UI drill-down always do). P&L and cash flow annual = sum of the year's months; balance sheet annual = the year's closing month. |
| Revenue | Total units per month (base units × seasonality index × growth) split by **mix shares** per product (must sum to 100% in every month; validated). Seasonality has one index per month, or, in a plan over 12 months, 12 indices repeated each plan year. `volume.annual_units` (key: the calendar year a fiscal year starts in) sets a whole fiscal year's units instead: units × index ÷ the year's sum of indices, to two decimals, the last month taking the remainder; a year without one keeps the growth rule. A year with annual units must be wholly inside the plan. Revenue = units × net price (list price × (1 − discount)). COGS = units × unit cost. |
| Payroll | Roster of hires: FTE, monthly salary, on-cost %, start month, optional end month. Cost = FTE × salary × (1 + on-cost) in active months. Paid in the same month. |
| Other opex | Named lines, fixed monthly amount from a start month. |
| Working capital | Collections curve: share of each month's revenue collected in month +0, +1, +2 … (sums to 100%). Supplier curve likewise for COGS and other opex. Opening AR and opening AP each have their own settlement schedule. |
| Capex | Items: amount, spend month (cash and fixed-asset cost), in-service month, useful life in months; straight-line depreciation from the in-service month. Opening fixed assets: cost, accumulated depreciation, remaining life → monthly depreciation = net ÷ remaining life. |
| Debt | Drawdowns and repayments by month. Interest = annual rate ÷ 12 × balance at the start of the month, expensed and paid in the same month. |
| Tax | Computed on **year-to-date PBT within the fiscal year** (the calendar year until planning part two; for a January start nothing changes). Tax for month m = rate × max(YTD PBT through m, 0), rounded to cents, − tax already booked YTD in that fiscal year. A later loss month therefore reverses earlier tax in the same year: monthly tax can be negative (a credit), and it is shown as booked. Over a fiscal year the tax equals rate × that year's PBT when it is positive, and zero when the year is a loss. Each fiscal year starts again from zero: **losses are not carried across fiscal years** (no loss relief), and a part first year starts its YTD at the first plan month. When a fiscal year in the plan has a loss before tax, a note says so in the checks (CLI, UI and the xlsx Checks sheet): "Tax: FY2027 has a loss before tax of (…); no tax is booked for it. No loss relief across fiscal years." The P&L line reads `Tax (17% of year-to-date profit)`, the rate taken from the plan. The payable is paid in full in the month after each calendar quarter ends; a negative payable (tax reversed after a quarter was paid) is refunded the same way. Unpaid tax is a liability. |
| Equity | Opening share capital and retained earnings; equity injections and dividends by month. The balance sheet shows share capital, **retained earnings brought forward** (opening + previous fiscal years' profit − dividends) and **profit for the year** (net income since the fiscal year started). In each fiscal year's first month the previous year's profit moves into retained earnings brought forward; total equity is unchanged by the move. |
| Checks (every month) | Assets = liabilities + equity exactly; cash-flow closing cash = balance-sheet cash; opening balance sheet balances. Mix shares 100% and settlement curves 100% are **validated on load** (a plan that fails them does not load), so they are listed as "(validated on load)", not as monthly computed results. Warnings: lowest month-end cash (over more than one fiscal year, also the lowest in each year), and CASH_SHORTFALL if any month-end cash < 0, in plain words: "Cash goes below zero from Mar-27 to Dec-27; lowest (144,150.00) in Mar-27" (each run of months named; a single month reads "in Mar-27"). A shortfall does not fail the checks; the UI banner then reads "Checks pass; cash warning" instead of "All checks pass", and the shortfall sentence sits next to the lowest-cash metric. |
| P&L-only mode | If the opening balance sheet is missing or incomplete, the engine says so plainly and produces the P&L only. It never invents a balance sheet. Fields an incomplete `[opening]` does give are still validated. Depreciation of the opening fixed assets stays in the P&L when `fixed_assets_cost`, `accumulated_depreciation` and `fixed_assets_remaining_life_months` are all given, and interest on the opening debt stays when `debt` is given; the P&L-only note says which of the two are included and, for any left out, which fields are missing. `fct plan compare` then reports "balance sheet checks not run (P&L-only)", never "all pass". |
| Scenario changes | What a scenario changes against base is listed one override per line, `collections.curve: 0/100 -> 0/50/50`, `hires.H2.start_month: 2027-04 -> 2027-06` (curves as shares joined by `/`; ASCII `->` because the CLI prints to a cp1252 Windows console). It is shown under the scenario selector in the UI, under the heading of `fct plan run` and `fct plan compare`, and as `scenario change` rows in the xlsx Assumptions sheet. The library computes it (`plan_config.scenario_changes`, carried on the model as `Model.changes`). |
| Month labels | `Jan-27` style everywhere: CLI, UI and the xlsx statement and schedule headers. |

## Data shapes

- **Plan** (`plans/<name>.toml`, versioned by content hash): `[setup]` entity, currency, first_month, months, optional fiscal_year_start; `[opening]` balance sheet; `[volume]` base_units, growth_pct_month, seasonality (one index per month, or 12 repeated each year), optional annual_units; `[[products]]` id, name, list_price, discount_pct, unit_cost, mix_pct; `[[hires]]`; `[[opex]]`; `[collections]` curve and opening AR schedule; `[payments]` curve and opening AP schedule; `[[capex]]`; `[debt]` rate_pct, drawdowns, repayments; `[tax]` rate_pct; `[equity]` injections, dividends; `[budget]` market and accounts (optional); `[scenarios.<name>]` overrides.
- **Scenario override**: a typed patch over the base (for example a different collections curve, a hire's start month, mix shares from a month, a price change from a month). Unknown keys are an error. Overrides are applied to a copy of the base assumptions; the base is never mutated.
- **Model output**: per month, the P&L lines, balance-sheet lines and cash-flow lines (operating, investing, financing), the supporting schedules (units and revenue by product, payroll by hire, fixed assets, debt, tax payable, AR and AP roll-forwards), the checks and warnings. Every statement line can name the schedule and assumption it came from (drill-down).

## Commands and UI

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `fct plan check PLAN` | Validates the plan file and lists errors and missing inputs |
| `fct plan run PLAN [--scenario S] [--months all]` | Prints the P&L, balance sheet and cash flow for Year 1 by month (every month with `--months all`) and per fiscal year, then the checks |
| `fct plan compare PLAN --scenario S [--months all]` | Scenario vs base: key lines by month and fiscal year (revenue, EBITDA, net income, closing cash, AR, lowest cash) |
| `fct plan export PLAN --out DIR [--scenario S]` | `plan_<name>_<scenario>.xlsx`: assumption sheets, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, schedules (every month, then the fiscal years), checks (values only, no formulas) |
| `fct plan to-budget PLAN --period YYYY-MM --out DIR [--scenario S] [--config close.toml]` | One plan month as the `[budget]` market's submission on the regional template (`docs/design-planning-2.md` B) |

UI: a **Planning** page: choose a plan file and scenario, see the three statements by month with a column per fiscal year, the checks (pass or fail, in words), the lowest cash (overall and per fiscal year), scenario vs base, drill from a statement line to its schedule (every month), and export a month as a budget submission. No business logic in the UI. Statement and schedule tables show every row with no inner scroll (height from the row count). The fiscal-year columns sit right after Line and all are pinned, so the line name and its annual figures stay in view while the months scroll sideways (the CLI and xlsx keep the fiscal years after the months).
